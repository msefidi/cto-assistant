Definitions and caveats
  • Cohort: B2C membership orders (no group or clinic membership) with status PAID, created in Europe/Berlin time within the cohort month, whose resulting user membership is in state NEWLY_CREATED. Renewals, revoked orders and admin-granted memberships are shown in the funnel but excluded.
  • First-time vs returning: returning members had at least one earlier user membership of any kind before the order.
  • Course pages before purchase: Segment page views tied to the account in the 24 hours before the order under /booking/events (course booking flow), /product or /content (catalogue and course pages). The membership checkout itself (/booking/memberships) is not counted.
  • First booking: earliest non-cancelled row in analysis.fct_course_bookings after the order. Almost all first-week bookings are livestream tickets for upcoming courses, paid with membership credit.
  • Web touch: earliest Segment page view in the 90 days before the order that carried a campaign tag or an external referrer. Browsing before login is not tied to the member, so absence of a touch is not evidence of a direct visit.
  • Stated source: analysis.fct_membership_marketing_sources; country-specific labels merged. Answered on all 166 orders of the August 2024 cohort.
  • Prices: EUR, CHF converted at 1.05. Promo code prefixes: PA = Postaussendung, BL = Beilage, INS = Inserat, MS = Messe/Stand.
  • Confidence: High = hard evidence or two independent signals agreeing; Medium = one observed signal, or a stated source on a fresh account; Low = stated only on an older account, or course browsing without a booking.
