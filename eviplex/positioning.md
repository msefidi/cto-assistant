# Eviplex: positioning

How Eviplex is positioned and sold. The wording itself (messages, voice, claims) is in meviq `brand/README.md`; quote it from there. This file holds what that book does not: markets, channels, competitors and the MDR line.

## Core message

> Eviplex, entwickelt für ärztliche Berufsgeheimnisträger, ermöglicht eine KI-gestützte Analyse internationaler Leitlinien und aktueller Studien für evidenzbasierte Antworten im medizinischen Alltag und bietet darüber hinaus themenbezogene FOMF Expertenvorträge an.

Promise on the website: „Evidenzbasierte Antworten in Sekunden – für Praxis und Klinik." Full message table and voice rules: meviq `brand/README.md`.

## What sets it apart (as published)

- Only released, validated sources, no free web search: 60,000+ international guidelines and curated current studies.
- FOMF expert lectures matched to the question, "a benefit exclusive to Eviplex".
- Built for physicians bound by professional secrecy (§ 203 StGB) and GDPR-compliant; Plus and Pro are the § 203 tiers.
- "Powered by FOMF", Europe's largest provider of continuing medical education.

Compare by category only (klinische Wissensportale, internationale KI-Tools, generische Chatbots), never by brand name, in anything published.

## FOMF proof points used in decks

- „Europas größter Anbieter für ärztliche Fortbildung mit über 85.000 Kursteilnahmen jährlich" (website, brand book).

## The MDR line

- Integrating into a KIS or PVS does not trigger the MDR on its own. Launching Eviplex from a practice system with a search term and no patient data (the AMBOSS-in-tomedo model) stays outside it.
- Reading patient context makes Eviplex a medical device (class IIa or IIb). The Plus/Pro Patient Context feature may already be close to that line; it is handed to the Linear project "MDR assessment" (P-EVP-245).
- Once patient data is processed in the cloud, § 393 SGB V asks for a C5 attestation (or ISO 27001 plus a remediation plan as a bridge), subprocessors included. OpenAI's C5 status is not known.
