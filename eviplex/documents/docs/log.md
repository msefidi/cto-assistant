## 2026-11-18: AlpineAI compared with Eviplex

From alpineai.swiss: AlpineAI AG (Davos, about 33 people) sells a general AI workspace, "SwissGPT". It offers chat, assistants built on your own documents (PDF upload, patient-dossier summaries), Swiss-German transcription, agents, and connectors to Swiss practice systems (Axenita, Aeskulap, INES, Logival, Mediway). It runs Mistral Large 3 and open models on its own hardware in Swiss data centres. It holds ISO 27001:2022 and VDSZ, and it offers an AVV and a professional-secrecy addendum. It sells Swiss HealthAssist with HIN (HIN eID only). It declares itself not a medical device, for administrative use only. Prices are CHF 80 and 90 per user per month, with Enterprise from 10 licences. It has no curated guideline or study base. (alpineai.swiss, read in session with Mahyar)
