The question keeps coming from sales and events: Prof. Luc Biedermann (CH) via Michi on 5 Nov 2026, then Gea Sokol (events) on 11 Nov 2026. On 11 Nov Mahyar answered Biedermann in Teams and forwarded the answer to Gea. He then planned an internal email to sales, events and B2B.

Agreed line (Mahyar, 11 Nov 2026):
- Today Eviplex is for evidence research in guidelines and studies. You can't upload PDFs, reports or lab results.
- Bringing patient documents into a query is on the roadmap, with no date. It is being built, like Plus/Pro, for Berufsgeheimnisträger under § 203 dStGB.
- Plus/Pro do not yet fully cover Art. 321 StGB (CH). The Google PSA only covers DE and AT; see [[eviplex-ch-tech-factsheet]].
- Images are not mentioned in the answer, so don't promise them.

**Why:** staff at events were improvising answers on a § 203 topic, and Eviplex GmbH carries the liability.
**How to apply:** reuse this wording for any upload or patient-data question. The Plus/Pro Patient Context feature does not include documents today either (Mahyar, 17 Nov 2026); an earlier note of 26 Oct that said it did was wrong. See [[eviplex-customer-support]].
