Every piece of work has exactly one Linear issue. If Mahyar does it himself (decide, ask, write, check), it goes on his private board `CTO`. If a product team builds it or needs to see it, it stays in that team's Linear (assigned to Mahyar if he does it) and is never duplicated on `CTO`. A topic's brief names its related team issues in its source map; Linear itself carries no cross-links from `CTO`. An issue with a folder (a topic) carries the `CTO` label *Folder*. Mahyar's Board ([[artifact-index]] row `my-board`) shows his `CTO` issues by stage next to his Outlook week, with his product-team issues folded below.

**Why:** decided 20 Nov 2026. A duplicate drifts (CTO-1 vs EVP-1314/1315 sat out of sync for three weeks), a link from the private board shows up on the team's issue, and removing team issues would cost the team visibility. The board is the backend for the topics Mahyar follows, not an inbox of everything.

**How to apply:** before filing Mahyar's work, ask "does a team need to see this?" Yes → team issue; no → `CTO`. When a topic gains or loses a related team issue, update its source map. Keep the Eviplex Watchlist out of his board; the Friday weekly reviews it.
