Mahyar plans his week and timeboxes by hand. On 25 Nov 2026 he chose a plain list of his open CTO issues by priority (details in a side panel) over a planning or timeboxing board; a Today view and a guided weekly ritual were prototyped and not adopted.

He looks at his board once, at the start of the day. What breaks his week: booked blocks that don't happen, and new work arriving mid-week, mostly promises he makes in email and Slack conversations. For now he registers those new items himself; no automatic detection from mail or Slack.

**Why:** he felt uncomfortable with a board that planned for him; he wants an overview, not a workflow.

**How to apply:** keep the board an overview. Later on 25 Nov 2026 he asked for quick edits on it: a clickable status icon and hover actions that set or clear the due date (Unschedule, This week, Today). Those stay; don't add planning, timeboxing or capture features unless he asks. See [[linear-one-issue-rule]].
