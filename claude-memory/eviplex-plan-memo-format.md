Mahyar posts a plan memo for the Eviplex team in the Eviplex weekly Teams meeting chat (English). Until 6 Nov 2026 it was per person. On 13 Nov 2026 he switched to a project-grouped format for both weekly and monthly plans, and edited the first weekly himself; his edited version is the template.

Template (see cto-assistant/eviplex-weekly/2026-11-16.md, one file per week named by the Monday):
- Title: "Plan for the coming week (dd.mm to dd.mm)"
- Numbered projects in roadmap order, bold name + owners in brackets
- Under each: "This week:" with 1–2 outcome bullets, then "Target: dd.mm" (project target date; "tbd" when unknown)
- "Side issues": one bullet per owner, topics comma-separated, owner in brackets
- "On the horizon" (things we might build): research/early meetings (e.g. MDR with Naeem, KIS)
- No separate "Mahyar" line; his items go under the project or the horizon section

His edits vs. the first draft: listed only the main owner per project (dropped secondary names like Max on regional content, Anika on cross-selling), added "Target" separate from "This week", moved his own meetings to "On the horizon".

**Why:** a per-person list scatters shared work and does not say what "done" looks like; project order matches the Friday weekly roadmap review.
**How to apply:** draft from EVP Linear projects (target dates) + current cycle, starting from the previous week's file. Keep security findings out of the chat and flag them separately. Keep it short, see [[comms-style-short]]; Linear setup in [[eviplex-linear-structure]].
