Answered 2026-10-13 for Mahyar (window Oct 2025–Sep 2026, DACH, staff excluded, new-customer rule from [[new-customer-sources]]). SQL in session scratchpad only (base CTEs = new_customers_by_source.sql + `fct_course_bookings` original_content_type='WEBINAR').

- WebUps are always €0: ~55k bookings, ~15.3k unique bookers; 53% already paying customers, 1,733 new accounts, 5,453 accounts that never paid.
- Of the ~7.2k non-payers, 334 later made a first purchase (164 membership, 170 course), €186k first-purchase value; 3.4% within 90 days.
- Accounts registered for a WebUp buy within 90 days at 4.0% vs 9.6% for accounts registered without any booking.
- Of 5,205 new paying customers, 368 (7%) had a WebUp as first-ever booking, but only 126 (2.4%) bought within 90 days of it; 160 (3%) registered for a WebUp. Matches Webinar-led = 1% of new members in [[b2c-value-journey-dashboard]].
- Conclusion: WebUps are sponsoring income plus engagement for existing customers, not acquisition. Results are correlations, so they are an upper bound on what WebUps cause.
