No serif display font for headings on pages built for Mahyar. He asked to "get rid of this font" for Instrument Serif headings on the board prototype (24 Nov 2026). Use the sans body font (e.g. Instrument Sans) at weight 600 for headings instead.

**Why:** he didn't like how it looked on his board.

**How to apply:** when building or updating his pages (board, analysis pages, explainers), don't load or use a serif display face for headings. Related: [[analysis-pages-style]].
