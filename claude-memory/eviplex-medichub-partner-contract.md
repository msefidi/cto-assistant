Rates changed to ranges by Mahyar (12 Nov): partner who brings the customer to closing 15–20% year 1, 10% year 2; pure introduction 5–10% year 1, and introduction applies only to hospitals/practices (an individual doctor, e.g. from Romania, counts only via the partner link as a referral). Rate within the range is set per partner by scope of work. Simulation still calculates at the top of the ranges.

Tech facts (meviq repo, 12 Nov 2026): paid plans are Stripe subscriptions only, no in-app purchase. UTM parameters are read at registration and sent to Segment only with analytics consent; they are not stored on the user account. So there is no billing-grade partner attribution yet.

My recommendation on 12 Nov (not yet decided by Mahyar): referral partner, not reseller; Eviplex GmbH is the contracting party; 20% of net collected revenue in a customer's first 12 paid months, 10% in months 13–24, then nothing; partner pays its own marketing; non-exclusive.

**Why:** Eviplex GmbH holds liability and is GDPR controller, so the partner must not sell, price or hold customer data.
**How to apply:** check what Mahyar decided before reusing these terms. Marketing claims from the partner need the approved wording in [[eviplex-ch-tech-factsheet]] ("zero data retention at the AI model provider").

First feedback on the concept (Mahyar, 12 Nov 2026 evening), applied to the doc, the Simulation tab and the Word file: introduction (Eviplex sells) = fixed 7.5% in months 1–12 (replaces 5–10%).
