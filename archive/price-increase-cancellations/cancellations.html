Renewal cancellations after the price notice

Since the notice emails started (sent every day to members whose renewal is exactly 60 days away), cancellations for renewals at the new price run about 30 above last year at the same point before renewal. For the renewals closest to their date (1–15 October) the total is below last year, so there the notice moved cancellations earlier. For late October and November it is too early to tell: last year most of their cancellations came in the final weeks, which are still ahead. Separately, more members cancel long before any notice, a trend that started in spring.

A separate trend: cancelling long before renewal

Share of memberships per renewal month whose renewal was switched off more than 90 days before the date, before any notice email.

This moved from about 7% to 10–11% with the April 2026 renewals, months before the price emails. It lifts the year-on-year comparison of total cancellations, so the price effect is read against the last 12 months, not against October 2025 alone.

What settles it

  1. Early November: the final cancellation rate of October renewals. If the late-October line flattens like the early one did, the notice changed timing only.
  2. From 1 October: withdrawals within 14 days of an automatic renewal at the new price. Members who ignore the email may react only when charged.
  3. Early December: the same curve for November renewals, then December once its notices go out.

How this is counted

Paid B2C memberships (no group or clinic membership; states newly created, renewed, paused), by scheduled renewal date. A cancellation is the first CANCEL_MEMBERSHIP request on the membership (public_membership_cancellation_request), dated by when it was made, counted only if made before the renewal date. A later switch back on still counts as a cancellation here.

The curve uses a survival count over days before renewal, so memberships that have not yet reached a given day drop out of that day's denominator instead of counting as not cancelled. Baseline months before Oct 2025 have too few memberships in this table to use.
