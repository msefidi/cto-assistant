How this is counted

  • People = Segment anonymous_id (one browser). The cohort is everyone whose first Registration Step · landing falls between 7 Jul and 6 Oct 2026. A later step counts if it happened within 30 days. 97% of completions happen within 30 minutes of step 1, and the median is 3 minutes.
  • Steps come from the Registration Step event, which fires when a screen is shown (RegistrationFormContainer.tsx). "Account created" is Signup Started, which fires after step 1 is submitted and just before the login redirect. "Complete" is the frontend Signup Completed.
  • Deepest step reached: a person who shows a later step is counted as having passed every earlier one. This covers events lost during the login redirect.
  • Step 4 (marketing source) went live on 27 Jul 2026. Before that, the form had three steps, and anyone who finished it counts as having passed step 4. Choose "4-step form only" to see the current flow without that assumption.
  • Crawlers removed: 9,045 visitor IDs in the window. About 7,700 are Baidu's renderer (Baiduspider-render). It loaded the page thousands of times on fomf.ch in July, on fomf.at on 27 Sep and on fomf.de on 14 Sep. The rest are IP addresses that opened the page as a "new visitor" 3+ times in one day. Only 6 of about 5,500 such visitors went past the email screen.
  • Logged in instead: the email screen rejects an address that already has an account. People who left there but fired a Login event within 24 hours very likely already had an account. They count as visitors but aren't lost registrations.
  • Coverage: Segment saw about 1,830 accounts created through this form. The user service recorded about 4,100 new self-registered accounts (no company link) in the same window. Two likely reasons, not yet checked: visitors who decline cookie consent aren't tracked, and some accounts come from other sign-up paths. Read the percentages as representative. Treat the absolute counts as a lower bound.
