Postaussendung · DACH · 1 January – 22 September, 2024 to 2026

What skipping the June mailing cost us

In 2024 and 2025 a mailing went out in June. In 2026 none went out between the end of March and 9 September. This page compares spend and return for the same window in each year, values new customers at their 24-month revenue by specialty and career stage, and estimates what the missing mailing lost.

    Spend = mailings that landed from 1 November of the year before until 22 September, so the pre-Christmas mailing that drives January bookings counts. Address purchases are not included. Value = 24-month revenue for their specialty, career stage and entry product.

    The June test: 2026 against the trend

    How this is counted, and what it cannot tell yet
    • New customer: first paid purchase ever (a paid single course, or a paid membership above €0), accounts created from March 2022 on, DACH, without FOMF staff. Free memberships offered by FOMF and revoked orders are not purchases. Same definition as the sources page.
    • 24-month value: revenue, not margin. Specialty uses the v2 profile field with the old field as fallback. Facharzt = occupation DOCTOR, Assistenzarzt = ASSISTANT_DOCTOR.
    • Costs: 2025 from “Postaussendung Kosten 2025” (marketing, per mailing, CHF × 1.05). 2026 DE mailings 2 and 3 from the print cost estimates in “Marketingplan_2026” (one repeated €43,325 print item counted once), AT from the same plan, DE mailing 1 and all CH 2026 estimated from 2025 cost per letter or the matching 2025 mailing. 2024 from the annual postal cost per country in the marketing cost master (EB PA analysis, kosten_kanal.csv), share of letters that landed in the window.
    • Autumn harvest is still open: summer mailings mostly sell autumn courses, booked from late August to November. The 9 September 2026 mailing has had two weeks. Repeat this comparison in December for 23 September – 30 November before calling it final.
    • The survey undercounts print: people give one answer, so a flyer that reminded someone who then booked after an email counts as email. The all-sources comparison in the June test does not depend on the survey. The EB PA analysis (recipient matching, Habib's team) attributes far more customers to post and finds no measurable effect of sending more often: reach and timing matter, repeat contacts do not.
    • Data: BigQuery fomf-dl (bookings, membership orders, both checkout surveys, analysis.print_campaigns); SharePoint Marketing and EB PA analysis files. Queried 24 Sep 2026. SQL in cto-assistant/archive/acquisition-sources/sql/, calculation in pa_analysis.py.
