Where new customers come from

New customers are people making their first paid purchase: a single course or a membership. Members answer “Mitgliedschaft” when they book, so that answer is spread over the other sources in the same proportions as the single-course buyers of that month and country.

    How this is counted
    • New customer: a user's first paid purchase ever. Only accounts created from March 2022 on are counted: older accounts were migrated from the old platform and their purchases before May 2022 are not in the data, which would make returning customers look new (more so in earlier years: 25% of 2023/24 against 12% of 2025/26). Single course = paid, non-member course booking with a price above €0. Membership = paid B2C membership order above €0 (no group or clinic membership). Free memberships offered by FOMF (14-month and 3-month free offers, about 100–300 a month since autumn 2025) and revoked orders are not purchases and are left out. Free webinars and free tickets do not make someone a customer. A single-course buyer who later becomes a member is counted once, as a single-course customer.
    • Source: the survey answer on the first paid booking. Member bookings are always answered “Mitgliedschaft”. New members are therefore counted from their membership order and spread over the sources by the single-course mix of the same month and country. Unanswered bookings (“No answer”) are shown but not used as a base.
    • Excluded: the UK, FOMF staff accounts, and answers “Test User” and “VA-Personal”.
    • First-purchase revenue: price of the first booking or of the first membership order, in EUR (CHF × 1.05). It is not lifetime value.
    • Caveats: excluding migrated accounts also drops some genuinely new customers who had an old account but never bought; that group shrinks over time. The membership survey was unanswered for 2% of new members in 2025/26 (1% in 2024/25, 13% in 2023/24). “FOMF website / former participant” was 10% of 2023/24 and almost disappears afterwards, because the option was dropped from the booking survey in April 2024 (bookings with that answer fell from about 500 a month to under 100); those people now pick other answers, so part of the 2023/24 → 2024/25 shift is the questionnaire. Survey labels differ by country and between the two surveys and were grouped by hand.
