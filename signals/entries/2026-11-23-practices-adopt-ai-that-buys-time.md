## Gist
Bördner gave the office-based physicians' view on a MEDICA 2026 panel. Practices use AI where it frees time for patients: speech recognition, letter writing, documentation, sorting appointment requests. Adoption is not broad yet, mainly because handoffs between care sectors carry no structured data: discharge letters arrive printed or as PDF and get retyped. He asks for three things: structured data as the standard (discharge letters, medication, ePA), a clear path from pilots into regular care and funding, and rollout support with reliable interfaces. The KV Nordrhein drives this through #Praxis4Future, a KI focus area that advises practices, and an AI pilot lab, "Mediteam Butzweilerhof". The medical decision stays with the physician.

## For FOMF
- **Eviplex value story.** The post names time saved as the adoption driver. Eviplex's published promise is speed („Evidenzbasierte Antworten in Sekunden"), but none of the named uses is evidence lookup; they are admin and documentation (`eviplex/knowledge/positioning.md`).
- **Documents.** The blocker he names (PDF discharge letters) is the same need Benignia reported for Swiss GP practices. Reading those letters is the GP-inbox use case, AlpineAI's ground, and patient context probably makes Eviplex a medical device (`eviplex/documents/`).
- **Interfaces.** "Reliable interfaces" matches the KIS/PVS research of 30 Oct 2026 (medatixx HealthHub, T2med pilot); launching Eviplex from a practice system without patient data stays outside the MDR.
- **Physician decides.** Matches Eviplex's own claim line: decision support, the doctor decides (`eviplex/knowledge/claims-and-legal.md`).
